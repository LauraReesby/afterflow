4Three alternatives to terracotta on the action surfaces

The pieces currently carrying terracotta: the Add button, the Save/Reflect buttons, the mood bar, the "Reflect" status tag and the mood-arc figure. Each option below moves the actions to a different voice and says what terracotta keeps. Reference at the far right is what's live now. Every value comes from the Organic ramps — sage is the system's declared second voice, chestnut is a deeper step of the same accent, ink is the neutral ramp.

4aSage-led

Actions and mood move to the sage ramp — the system's genuine second voice. Terracotta retreats to state only: the "Reflect" tag and the mood-lift line on Trends. Calmer, more clinical, further from the warm-orange app.
